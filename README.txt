WEBSITE CONCEPT AND THEME
For this assignment, I created an event information page for SET Days 2026
on December 2-4, 2026 at National University Fairview. My website title is
"SET Days 2026" with the tagline "Rise. Compete. Unite."

The concept focuses on bringing students from different programs together
through sports, esports, quiz bees, and workshops. For the theme, I used
National University's blue and yellow colors on a clean white background
with Arial text so the sections, schedule table, and activity boxes are
clear and easy to follow.


WEBSITE CONTENT
Everything is organized on one page (index.html) with internal navigation
links jumping to each section: Home, About SET Days, Proposed Event
Activities, Three-Day Event Schedule, Event Venue and Interactive Guide,
Featured Activities and Guests, Participation Guidelines, and Contact
Information. The campus picture in the Venue section also works as an
image map that links to major sections of the page.

Note: All schedules, activities, guest speaker details, committee names,
and contact information on the site are fictional and made up for this
class activity.
